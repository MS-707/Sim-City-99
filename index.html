ℹ️ About SimCity 99✕

SimCity 99 — The 1997 Edition

A love letter to isometric city builders and the year 1997.
All graphics are drawn procedurally on canvas and all audio is
synthesized live with WebAudio. No copyrighted assets inside.

Tip: hold right mouse button to pan, scroll to zoom.
Keys 1–9,0,-,=,s,h select tools • Space pauses.
Touch screens: tap or drag one finger to build, two-finger drag
to pan the map, pinch to zoom.
Click a citizen complaint on the news ticker to jump straight
to the offending block.
